1. All components are used unmodified. / 上述组件均原样使用、未修改。

   redistribute the binaries, comply with their respective licenses: aria2 is GPL-2.0-or-later,
   and **this FFmpeg build is GPL-3.0** (its build enables --enable-gpl --enable-version3).
   Ship the license texts (in this folder) and give recipients a way to obtain the corresponding
   source. For unmodified builds, the official source locations above (and the FFmpeg build page)
   satisfy this in practice.

     请随附许可文本并指明对应源码获取方式；未修改的构建以官方源码地址（及 gyan.dev 构建页）为准。
