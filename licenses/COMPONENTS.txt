Redistribution notes / 再分发注意
1. All components are used unmodified. / 上述组件均原样使用、未修改。
2. aria2 and FFmpeg are distributed as standalone executables invoked as processes. When you
   redistribute the binaries, comply with GPL-2.0/LGPL-2.1: ship the license texts (in this
   folder) and give recipients a way to obtain the corresponding source. For unmodified builds,
   the official source locations above (and the FFmpeg build page) satisfy this in practice.
   / aria2 与 FFmpeg 为随包二进制（进程调用）。再分发时请随附许可文本并指明对应源码获取方式；
     未修改的构建以官方源码地址（及 gyan.dev 构建页）作为对应源码的提供方式。
3. MIT / BSD-3 / Apache-2.0 / PSF components: keep the copyright and license notices with the
   distribution (see files in this folder). / MIT/BSD/Apache/PSF 组件：随分发保留版权与许可声明。
4. License texts are provided for reference; the upstream official texts govern.
   / 许可文本仅供参考，以各组件上游官方文本为准。
