My shares

Select files in Browse and press Share in the toolbar: they are mapped into public/shares// — visitors can browse and download them without signing in. The original files are never moved, so a share stops working the moment its source file is deleted, renamed or moved; a file published from the public folder public/ can be deleted by other users too.
